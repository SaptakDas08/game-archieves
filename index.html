About me

I build Game Archieves in the open — a small, hand-checked catalogue of games worth knowing about, with the requirements and the official place to get each one.

Every entry here is added by hand and only from sources I can verify. The site hosts no game files: it links to the official store, the developer, or a download the rights holder has expressly authorized. Found a mistake? The repository is public.
